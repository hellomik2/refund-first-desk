Who runs this. An operator of AI shopping agents can run this desk on PayPal today: it receives the payment, refunds from it, and makes an innocent store whole from its own funds. If PayPal ran it itself, it would hold the store's funds for the disputed order instead (delayed disbursement exists for approved partners).

What is only true in the sandbox. Charging a saved wallet again without the buyer present needs PayPal's approval on live accounts. "Refunded" here means PayPal's refund status is COMPLETED; when the money shows up depends on the buyer's funding source.
