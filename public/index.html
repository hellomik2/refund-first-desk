Refund-First Desk

Trust the buyer first. If that was wrong, give notice and take it back.

For purchases made by AI shopping agents, on PayPal.

PayPal sandbox
  1. 1 Refund first The PayPal refund completes in seconds, before anything is checked. Within the limits ($50 a case, 2 refunds per 30 days). Sandbox runs: 1.87–3.17 s.
  2. 2 Then check The AI and fixed rules in code read the records. Money moves only when they give the same answer. If they differ, a person decides and nothing is charged meanwhile.
  3. 3 Take it back only if the buyer was responsible Notice first, 5 business days to dispute, then the wallet that paid is charged again, never more than was refunded. A dispute stops it. In every other ending the buyer keeps the refund.

Other entries in this hackathon also check agent purchases against what the buyer approved. Among the 217 we read, we did not find one that refunds before the check and has a step to take the money back. PayPal's own buyer protection terms already allow a temporary refund that is recovered, after at least 5 business days' notice, if a dispute goes against the buyer; this desk applies that to agent purchases, by rule.

How the careful and the hasty agent differ, and what is in the catalog

Same model (claude-haiku-5-5) and settings. Two things differ, and nothing else:

  • What it is told. Careful: the approved conditions bind; open the product page of anything it adds; compare the cart total with the limit before paying; do not pay without permission. Hasty: it is an express-checkout agent graded on speed; pick from the top of the results and finish with payment.
  • What it can see. Careful: the buyer's words and the approved condition slots; search results with store, variant (scent, connector, size) and shipping; a product page and a cart view with shipping and tax. Hasty: the buyer's words only; search results with product number, listing title and price; no product page and no cart view.

Neither is told to make a mistake, and no mistake is written in by code: whatever it buys, it chose. The catalog is made so a mistake is possible: a cheaper copy whose shipping and tax push the total over the limit, listing titles that do not show the scent or the connector, multi-packs, and sponsored listings shown first.

Money who holds what

Who runs this. An operator of AI shopping agents can run this desk on PayPal today: it receives the payment, refunds from it, and makes an innocent store whole from its own funds. If PayPal ran it itself, it would hold the store's funds for the disputed order instead (delayed disbursement exists for approved partners).

What the AI does and does not do. The quick check can only close a case in the buyer's favour. The investigator names the first step where the agent left the mandate and quotes it; code checks the quote against that step. Its verdict is one of two keys, and money moves only when the rules key gives the same answer. When they differ, a person decides.

What is only true in the sandbox. Charging a saved wallet again without the buyer present needs PayPal's approval on live accounts. "Refunded" here means PayPal's refund status is COMPLETED; when the money shows up depends on the buyer's funding source.
